We take hardware off the founder's plate. We are tech-savvy B.Tech grads who right-size laptops and IT gear by role (e.g. don't buy a MacBook for a documentation person, but do give a video editor a strong machine). Warranty-backed, one point of contact, low commission, no running around markets like Nehru Place.
